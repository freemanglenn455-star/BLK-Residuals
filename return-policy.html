RETURN & REFUND POLICY

Last updated September 17, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with your BLK Residuals purchase. Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Return eligibility

Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, and returned in their original condition with original tags and packaging. Proof of purchase is required. Items showing wear, damage, alterations, odors, or missing tags may not be accepted.

Exchanges

Exchanges are accepted for eligible items, subject to inventory availability. Limited-release products may sell out before an exchange can be completed.

Refunds

After an eligible return is received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are not refundable unless required by law or the return is due to our error. Your bank or card issuer may require additional time to post the refund.

Final-sale and non-returnable items

Items clearly marked final sale, gift cards, and customized or personalized products are not returnable except where required by law.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact BLK Residuals promptly through the support contact information shown at checkout so we can review the issue.

Legal rights

This policy does not limit rights or remedies that cannot be waived under applicable law.
